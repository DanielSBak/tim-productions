The Vision

Based in Sacramento, California, Tim sees the world not in static frames, but in fluid moments. Like liquid silver, light bends, shifts, and shapes our reality. Every photograph is a drop of time, suspended forever. He specializes in capturing the raw, the real, and the unspoken emotions that flow between us.
